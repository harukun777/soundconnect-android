# Android 9 port brief

Audience: Japanese-speaking headphone owners using Android 9 or newer.
System: adopt the existing charcoal/gold SoundConnect palette; Android native widgets and Roboto/Noto Sans.
Remember: choose the headphones, then directly control the supported sound settings.
Register: R1 Utility, because reliability and readable controls on older phones take priority.
Interaction: I3 control application, with live Bluetooth state and committed device settings.
Scope: compact scrollable native screen; no promotional page or invented product claims.
Assumptions: Japanese primary language; existing branding retained; foreground connection only.
